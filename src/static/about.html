About us

Helping seniors claim what they've already earned

Senior Daily Benefits exists to close one gap: the distance between the programs that are available to Americans over 60 and the people who never hear about them.

Our story

Senior Daily Benefits was founded on a simple but powerful realization: millions of American seniors are missing out on benefits they're entitled to, simply because they don't know these programs exist.

Our research found that the average senior household leaves more than $1,800 in benefits unclaimed each year. That isn't because people don't qualify. It's because the information is scattered across federal, state, and local agencies, written in language nobody enjoys reading, and rarely reaches the people it's meant for.

So we built a free 60-second quiz that asks a few plain questions about your situation and points you to the programs most likely to apply. Then we started writing a daily brief that explains, in plain English, what changed and what to do about it.

Why we do this

Every senior deserves to live with dignity and financial security. After decades of contributing through work, taxes, and community, the least the system can do is make its own programs easy to find.

Unfortunately, it isn't. Benefits are scattered across federal, state, and local programs. Eligibility rules vary. Application processes are confusing. Many people give up before they start, or never know there was something to start.

That's where we come in. We do the research, simplify the process, and put the information where you can actually use it. If a single article or quiz result helps one more person claim something they'd been missing, the day's work was worth it.
