How we work

Three rules, every article, every issue.

📎

Primary sources

Numbers come from the Social Security Administration, Medicare, the IRS, the CBO, the BLS and company filings, and every article links to them.

🗣️

Opinions are labeled

When we take a side we say "we think." When a famous person is right, we say so. When they're selling something, we say that too.

🏷️

Sponsors are marked

Partner offers are labeled and never change what we recommend. We may earn a commission if you sign up through them.
